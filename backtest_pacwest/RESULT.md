## Reading
- Third case and second blind test. **Survival direction: right in both blind tests** (CS, PacWest), and the failure case (SVB) also fails.
- **Size bias:** the v3 large-account agents overshoot. In the CS development pass it was 8% vs 5%; here, blind, 22% vs 7%. Large accounts hold 30% of PacWest deposits and the engine moves 19 points of it, nearly two-thirds of that money. Venture-banking agents read SVB-style danger into a bank that was 73–75% insured with liquidity of about 188% of uninsured deposits.
- The playbook signals don't include insured share or liquidity coverage, so a mostly-insured bank looks to the agents just like SVB. That is the likely next fix: add bank-fact signals to the playbook state (fixed per bank, not tuned to outcomes).
- Counterfactual ranking (engine): early backstop 11% withdrawn > transparent liquidity 21% ≈ actual statement 22% > silence 28%.
