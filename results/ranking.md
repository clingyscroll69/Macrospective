## Why this order

R0 (0.04) and R1 (0.02) are close to hopeless; the R0/R1 CIs are small, so their order is the engine's, not a strong separation, and I do not overrule it.

Every response still fails mostly at the Mar 9 06:00 PT wire open: typical failure hour is Mar 9 06:00 PT for all five, and the 06:00 wire-open bucket is the largest failure type in each (R4 0.29, R3 0.41, R2 0.59, R0 0.68, R1 0.73). The implication for timing: reassurance has to reach SF_Bay_Area before the wires open, since by then alarm (peak Mar 8 22:00 PT in most arms) has already built. R1's 11:00 PT Mar 9 call lands after the typical failure, which is why it ranks last. Earlier and stronger beats later and softer, but even R4 does not remove the 06:00 risk.
